Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

ResearchMed Connect ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through researchmed.in, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

1. What we collect

We only collect information that you choose to give us when you send an enquiry through the contact form or the chat assistant:

  • Your name, email address and (optionally) phone / WhatsApp number
  • Your qualification, designation and institution (optional)
  • The service you are interested in and the message you write

We do not ask for, and you should not send, sensitive details such as health records, identity numbers or bank details through the website.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a confirmation email
  • To provide the guidance or authorship opportunity you asked about
  • To keep a record of our communication with you

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Your consent

By submitting the contact form or sending an enquiry through the chat assistant, you consent to us using your details for the purposes above. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; this will not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

4. Services that help us run the website

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers enquiries from the form and chat assistant to our inbox and sends your confirmation email.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our email inbox, where enquiries are stored.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may record technical data such as IP addresses in server logs for security.
  • jsDelivr (a public content network) delivers the website's fonts, so your browser connects to its servers when pages load.
  • WhatsApp is used only if you choose to message us there, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

5. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The website stores small preferences in your own browser (for example, light or dark mode, and whether you have already seen the chat greeting). These never leave your device.

To count visitors, we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that sets no cookies and does not collect personal information. It records anonymous totals such as page views, the pages visited, the referring website, and the visitor's country, browser and screen size. We use these totals only to understand how the website is used.

6. How long we keep data

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to provide our services, and generally no longer than two years after our last contact with you, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you
  • correct or update it
  • delete it
  • stop contacting you

Email info@researchmed.in with the subject "Privacy request". We aim to respond within 30 days.

8. Security

The website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to our inbox and website administration is restricted. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Children

Our services are intended for students, professionals and researchers aged 18 and above. If you are under 18, please contact us with the involvement of a parent, guardian or your institution.

10. Grievances and contact

If you have a question or complaint about how your data is handled, please write to our grievance contact, the Managing Director, ResearchMed Connect, at info@researchmed.in.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
