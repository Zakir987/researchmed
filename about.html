About us

Your partner in research, learning and publication

ResearchMed Connect is an educational and academic guidance platform created to support people through every stage of their research and publication journey.

We understand that research can feel complicated, especially for students and early-career professionals. Our goal is to make research concepts, academic resources and publication processes easier to understand and more accessible.

We aim to support learning, encourage ethical research practices, and provide affordable guidance to anyone who wants to strengthen their research and academic skills.

Who we support

  • Healthcare professionals who wish to explore research, academic writing and publication opportunities.
  • Students building confidence with research concepts and scientific enquiry.
  • Researchers looking for guidance and resources at different stages of a project.
  • Aspiring authors who want to organise, prepare and publish a book.

Our approach

We focus on teaching, not ghost-writing. Every piece of guidance is meant to help you understand the process and produce original, ethical work of your own.
