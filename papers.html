Research collaborations

Collaborate on a research study

We invite healthcare professionals, educators, students and researchers to join our studies from the planning stage. Authorship is earned through real contribution, following ICMJE authorship criteria.

How authorship works

Every author on our papers must meet all four ICMJE criteria. We do not offer authorship on papers that are already written.

  1. Contribute to the workLiterature search, data collection, analysis or interpretation.
  2. Write or reviseDraft a section or critically revise the manuscript.
  3. Approve the final versionRead and approve what is submitted.
  4. Take responsibilityBe accountable for the accuracy and integrity of your part.
