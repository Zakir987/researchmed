Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our guidance and resources.

Do you write research papers for clients?

No. We provide educational and editorial guidance so you can produce your own original work. We encourage ethical research and responsible authorship.

Who will guide me?

Well-known professionals from allied health domains. Every mentor holds a Ph.D. and has more than 7 years of experience in teaching and research, and we match you with a mentor from your own field.

Which subjects do you cover?

Allied health and health sciences, including anaesthesia and OT technology, medical laboratory technology, radiology and imaging, physiotherapy, nursing, cardiac care, dialysis, emergency care, respiratory therapy, optometry, public health and health professions education.

Is my work kept confidential?

Yes. Your topic, data and drafts are used only to guide you and are never shared or reused.

Do you guarantee publication?

No. Publication decisions are made by journals and their peer reviewers. We help you prepare a stronger manuscript and understand the process.

Which journals do you guide authors towards?

We help you shortlist suitable, ethical journals, including those indexed in Scopus, Embase, PubMed and Web of Science, and avoid predatory journals.

Do you offer video lectures or live sessions?

Yes, on request. Tell us the topic through the enquiry form and we will arrange a recorded or live session for you or your group.

Who can use ResearchMed Connect?

Healthcare professionals, students, researchers and aspiring authors at any stage.

How do I get one-to-one guidance?

Use the contact page to send us a message by email or WhatsApp, and tell us briefly what you need.

Ready to begin your research journey?

Tell us about your research, manuscript or book. We reply to every enquiry, usually within one to two working days.
