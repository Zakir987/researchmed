Section 01

Peer review process

  1. Initial check

    ~7 days

    Scope, completeness, similarity check and ethics statements.

  2. Peer review

    Double-blind

    At least two independent reviewers with relevant expertise. Reviewers and authors do not know each other's identities.

  3. Decision

    6–8 weeks

    Accept, minor revision, major revision or reject, with the reviewers' comments.

  4. Revision

    Point by point

    Authors reply to each comment. Revised papers may go back to the reviewers.

  5. Publication

    Online

    Copy-editing, proofs sent to the corresponding author, then online publication.

⏱️

We aim to give a first decision within 6–8 weeks of submission. Reviewers must declare conflicts of interest and keep manuscripts confidential.

Section 03

Publication ethics

🧭COPE flowcharts

We follow COPE flowcharts to handle suspected plagiarism, duplicate publication, data fabrication or falsification, image manipulation, authorship disputes and undisclosed conflicts of interest.

🔍Similarity & plagiarism

Manuscripts with substantial unattributed overlap with other work are returned or rejected.

👥Honest authorship

Authorship must meet the ICMJE criteria. Gift, guest and ghost authorship are not accepted. Changes to the author list after submission need a signed request from all authors.

🔒Confidentiality

Editors and reviewers must not use unpublished information from a manuscript for their own work.

Section 04

Editorial independence & conflicts of interest

The journal is published by ResearchMed Connect, which also offers research and publication guidance. To keep decisions independent:

⚖️No advantage

Using ResearchMed Connect's guidance services gives no advantage in review or acceptance, and is never a condition of publication.

📢Declared up front

Authors must declare at submission if they received guidance from ResearchMed Connect. Such manuscripts are handled by an editor with no involvement in that guidance.

🧑‍⚖️Editors' own papers

Manuscripts authored by an editor, a board member or the publisher's staff are handled entirely by another editor and independent reviewers. The author has no access to the review.

🚪Stepping aside

Editors and reviewers step aside from any manuscript where they have a personal, professional or financial conflict.

Section 05

Corrections, retractions & expressions of concern

✏️Corrections

Honest errors are corrected with a published correction linked to the original article.

⚠️Retractions

Articles with serious problems (for example, unreliable data or misconduct) are retracted following COPE's retraction guidelines; the article stays online, clearly marked as retracted, with the reason.

Section 06

Appeals & complaints

30days

Appeal a decision

Authors may appeal a decision once, by email, within 30 days, explaining why they believe it was wrong. Appeals are reviewed by an editor not involved in the original decision.

7days

Raise a complaint

Complaints about the journal's processes can be sent to the same address and are acknowledged within 7 days.

Write to the editorial office: ijaott@researchmed.in
