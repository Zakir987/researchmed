Editorial policies

How we review, publish and correct the scholarly record. We follow the guidance of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and the ICMJE Recommendations.

Peer review process

  1. Initial check (about 7 days): scope, completeness, similarity check and ethics statements.
  2. Double-blind peer review: at least two independent reviewers with relevant expertise. Reviewers and authors do not know each other's identities.
  3. Decision: accept, minor revision, major revision or reject, with the reviewers' comments.
  4. Revision: authors reply to each comment point by point. Revised papers may go back to the reviewers.
  5. Publication: copy-editing, proofs sent to the corresponding author, then online publication.

We aim to give a first decision within 6–8 weeks of submission. Reviewers must declare conflicts of interest and keep manuscripts confidential.

Open access & licence

All articles are free to read on publication. Articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) licence: anyone may share and adapt the work with proper credit. Authors keep the copyright to their work.

Publication ethics

  • We follow COPE flowcharts to handle suspected plagiarism, duplicate publication, data fabrication or falsification, image manipulation, authorship disputes and undisclosed conflicts of interest.
  • Manuscripts with substantial unattributed overlap with other work are returned or rejected.
  • Authorship must meet the ICMJE criteria. Gift, guest and ghost authorship are not accepted. Changes to the author list after submission need a signed request from all authors.
  • Editors and reviewers must not use unpublished information from a manuscript for their own work.

Editorial independence and conflicts of interest

The journal is published by ResearchMed Connect, which also offers research and publication guidance. To keep decisions independent:

  • Using ResearchMed Connect's guidance services gives no advantage in review or acceptance, and is never a condition of publication.
  • Authors must declare at submission if they received guidance from ResearchMed Connect. Such manuscripts are handled by an editor with no involvement in that guidance.
  • Manuscripts authored by an editor, a board member or the publisher's staff are handled entirely by another editor and independent reviewers. The author has no access to the review.
  • Editors and reviewers step aside from any manuscript where they have a personal, professional or financial conflict.

Corrections, retractions and expressions of concern

Honest errors are corrected with a published correction linked to the original article. Articles with serious problems (for example, unreliable data or misconduct) are retracted following COPE's retraction guidelines; the article stays online, clearly marked as retracted, with the reason.

Appeals and complaints

Authors may appeal a decision once, by email, within 30 days, explaining why they believe it was wrong. Appeals are reviewed by an editor not involved in the original decision. Complaints about the journal's processes can be sent to the same address and are acknowledged within 7 days.

Email: info@researchmed.in

Archiving, advertising and privacy

Published articles and their PDFs are kept permanently on this website. Each article page shows its publication history and how to cite it. The journal carries no advertising. Names and email addresses entered here are used only for the journal's work and are never shared or sold — see our Privacy Policy.
