About the journal

The Indian Journal of Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology (IJAOTT) publishes research and educational work by and for anaesthesia technologists, operation theatre technologists, perioperative nurses, anaesthesiologists, surgeons and allied health educators. It is published by ResearchMed Connect.

Every manuscript is checked for originality and sent for double-blind peer review. Accepted articles are published open access, free to read, under the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) licence.

Aims

  • Publish sound, ethical research that improves the safety and quality of anaesthesia and operation theatre care.
  • Give allied health professionals and students a dedicated, peer-reviewed home for their work.
  • Strengthen evidence-based practice and education in anaesthesia and OT technology.

Scope

  • Anaesthesia technology, equipment and monitoring
  • Airway management and difficult-airway preparedness
  • Operation theatre practice, workflow and safety
  • Sterilisation, asepsis and infection control
  • Perioperative and post-anaesthesia care
  • Patient safety, checklists and human factors
  • Critical care, ventilation and respiratory support
  • Pain management and regional anaesthesia
  • Biomedical equipment in the OT and ICU
  • Ergonomics and occupational health in the OT
  • Education, simulation and competency assessment
  • Emergency, trauma and disaster preparedness
