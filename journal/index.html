Our aims

Why IJAOTT exists

A dedicated journal for the people who keep operating theatres safe.

🛡️

Safer patient care

Publish sound, ethical research that improves the safety and quality of anaesthesia and operation theatre care.

🎓

A home for allied health

Give allied health professionals and students a dedicated, peer-reviewed home for their work.

📈

Evidence into practice

Strengthen evidence-based practice and education in anaesthesia and OT technology.

Scope

What we publish

Original research, reviews, case reports, short communications and educational articles in these areas.

  • 🩺Anaesthesia technology, equipment & monitoring
  • 🫁Airway management & difficult airway
  • 🏥OT practice, workflow & safety
  • 🧼Sterilisation, asepsis & infection control
  • 🛏️Perioperative & post-anaesthesia care
  • ✅Patient safety, checklists & human factors
  • 💨Critical care, ventilation & respiratory support
  • 💉Pain management & regional anaesthesia
  • ⚙️Biomedical equipment in the OT & ICU
  • 🧍Ergonomics & occupational health
  • 🧑‍🏫Education, simulation & competency
  • 🚑Emergency, trauma & disaster preparedness
