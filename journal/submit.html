What happens next

  1. You get a reference number on screen and by email.
  2. Use it on Track manuscript to see your paper's status.
  3. We check the submission within about 7 days.
  4. Double-blind peer review; first decision in about 6–8 weeks.
  5. APC (₹2,000 – ₹5,000) is decided only after acceptance.
