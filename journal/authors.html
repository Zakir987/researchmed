Section 02

Preparing your manuscript

📄Files

Upload the manuscript as a Word file (.doc or .docx, up to 10 MB). Put the title page as the first page; the editorial office removes it before the manuscript goes to reviewers. Download the manuscript template.

🏷️Title page

Title, running title, all authors with qualifications and affiliations, ORCID iDs, corresponding author's email and phone, word count, and the declarations listed below.

🕶️Anonymised manuscript

No author names, institutions or acknowledgements that would reveal identity.

📐Layout

A4, 12-point font, 1.5 line spacing, numbered pages and lines.

🖼️Tables & figures

Number them in order of citation. Figures at 300 dpi (JPG/PNG/TIFF). Do not show patient faces or identifying details.

🔤Units & abbreviations

SI units; define each abbreviation at first use.

Structure (original research)

  1. Title
  2. Abstract
  3. 3–6 keywords
  4. Introduction
  5. Methods
  6. Results
  7. Discussion
  8. Conclusion
  9. References

Abstract headings: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion.

Section 03

Ethics & registration

🏛️Ethics committee approval

Studies involving people or patient records need approval from an Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC). State the committee and approval number in Methods.

✍️Informed consent

State how informed consent was obtained. Case reports need written consent from the patient (or guardian) for publication.

🧾Trial registration

Clinical trials must be registered with the Clinical Trials Registry – India (CTRI) or another WHO-recognised registry before the first participant is enrolled. Give the registration number.

🌐Guidelines followed

Research must follow the Declaration of Helsinki and the ICMR National Ethical Guidelines.

🤝
Section 04

Authorship & declarations

👥Authorship (ICMJE)

Substantial contribution to the work; drafting or critically revising it; approving the final version; and agreeing to be accountable for it. Include a short contribution statement for each author.

⚖️Conflicts & funding

Conflicts of interest and funding must be declared, even if there are none.

🤖Use of AI tools

Say where and how AI tools were used (for example, language editing). AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and authors are responsible for all content.

🔍Originality

The manuscript must not be published or under review elsewhere. All submissions are checked for similarity.

🗂️Data availability

State whether and how the data can be accessed.

Section 05

References

Use the Vancouver style: number references in the order they are first cited, with numbers in square brackets [1]. List up to six authors, then "et al." Give the DOI where available.

FormatAuthor A, Author B, Author C. Title of the article. Abbreviated Journal Title. Year;Volume(Issue):first–last page. doi:…
  • Authors (up to 6, then et al.)
  • Article title
  • Journal (abbreviated)
  • Year; volume (issue): pages
  • DOI

Section 06

Fees

To submit

₹0

No fee to submit or for peer review.

After acceptance

APC

Article processing charge (APC): ₹2,000 – ₹5,000, decided only after a manuscript is accepted, depending on the article type and length. Nothing is charged before acceptance.

Can't pay?

Waiver

Authors who cannot pay may request a waiver; editors are not told whether a waiver was requested.

⚖️

Fees never influence editorial decisions.
