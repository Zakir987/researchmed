Preparing your manuscript

  • Files: send the manuscript as an editable Word file (.docx). Send the title page separately so the main manuscript can be anonymised for peer review.
  • Title page: title, running title, all authors with qualifications and affiliations, ORCID iDs, corresponding author's email and phone, word count, and the declarations listed below.
  • Anonymised manuscript: no author names, institutions or acknowledgements that would reveal identity.
  • Layout: A4, 12-point font, 1.5 line spacing, numbered pages and lines.
  • Structure (original research): Title, Abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion), 3–6 keywords, Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References.
  • Tables and figures: number them in order of citation. Figures at 300 dpi (JPG/PNG/TIFF). Do not show patient faces or identifying details.
  • Units and abbreviations: SI units; define each abbreviation at first use.
  • Reporting guidelines: follow CONSORT (trials), STROBE (observational studies), PRISMA (systematic reviews), CARE (case reports) or STARD (diagnostic accuracy) as relevant.

Ethics & registration

  • Studies involving people or patient records need approval from an Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC). State the committee and approval number in Methods.
  • State how informed consent was obtained. Case reports need written consent from the patient (or guardian) for publication.
  • Clinical trials must be registered with the Clinical Trials Registry – India (CTRI) or another WHO-recognised registry before the first participant is enrolled. Give the registration number.
  • Research must follow the Declaration of Helsinki and the ICMR National Ethical Guidelines.

Authorship & declarations

  • Authorship follows the ICMJE criteria: substantial contribution to the work; drafting or critically revising it; approving the final version; and agreeing to be accountable for it. Include a short contribution statement for each author.
  • Conflicts of interest and funding must be declared, even if there are none.
  • Use of AI tools: say where and how AI tools were used (for example, language editing). AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and authors are responsible for all content.
  • Originality: the manuscript must not be published or under review elsewhere. All submissions are checked for similarity.
  • Data availability: state whether and how the data can be accessed.

References

Use the Vancouver style: number references in the order they are first cited, with numbers in square brackets [1]. List up to six authors, then "et al." Give the DOI where available.

Format: Author A, Author B, Author C. Title of the article. Abbreviated Journal Title. Year;Volume(Issue):first–last page. doi:…

Fees

There is no fee to submit a manuscript.

Fees never influence editorial decisions. Authors who cannot pay may request a waiver when they submit; editors are not told whether a waiver was requested.

Submission checklist

  • Title page (separate file) with all authors, affiliations and the corresponding author
  • Anonymised manuscript in Word format
  • Ethics approval number and consent statement (where applicable)
  • Trial registration number (for clinical trials)
  • Conflict of interest, funding, AI-use and author contribution statements
  • References in Vancouver style
  • Tables and figures cited in the text
